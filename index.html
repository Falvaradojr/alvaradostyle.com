Alvarado Style Services/Structural

Structural

Beams, headers and the load path, built to the engineer's drawings. LVL beams, flitch plates and structural headers, supplied or installed, for contractors, builders and homeowners.

Get a quoteCall 732-470-1583

Three ways to work with us

Supply it, install it, or run the whole scope

Contractors and builders can bring us one piece of their job. Homeowners can hand us the whole wall. Either way, the beam is built to the drawings.

Beam supply

You have the drawings and your own crew. We put together the beam package to the plan and get it to the site.

  • LVL plies, flitch plate steel, hangers and posts
  • Multi-ply beams assembled to spec
  • Jobsite delivery through Supply Run, our delivery company

Fabricate & install

We build the beam, shore the structure, open the wall and set it, then carry the load down to the foundation.

  • Temporary shoring before anything comes out
  • Beam, posts, hangers and bearing set and inspected
  • Framing left ready for the next trade

Full structural scope

Wall removal through finish, with our other divisions on the same schedule.

  • Demo opens the wall
  • Structural sets the beam
  • Concepts closes it up and finishes the room

Products and services

What we build and set

LVL & glulam beams

Laminated veneer lumber in 1¾ in. plies, built up 2-, 3- or 4-ply, depths from 9¼ to 18 in. Set flush or dropped.

Flitch plates

A steel plate bolted between lumber plies for long spans and heavy loads where an LVL alone will not do.

Structural headers

Headers for new and widened window and door openings, with jack and king studs sized to the load.

Posts, hangers & bearing

Point loads carried through posts, hangers and bearing plates down to a footing that can take them.

Temporary shoring

Shoring walls set before anything comes out, so the house is never carrying itself on hope.

Structural repairs

Sagging floors, sistered joists, framing corrections and foundation work where the load path has failed.
